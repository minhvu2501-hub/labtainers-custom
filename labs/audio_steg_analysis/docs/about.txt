Audio Steganography Analysis Lab

This lab teaches students to perform digital audio forensics by analyzing
four anonymous WAV audio files to identify which steganographic technique
(if any) was used to hide data within each file.

Students will use statistical analysis tools (LSB distribution, chi-square
tests, MSE, SNR, Shannon entropy) to distinguish:
  - The original unmodified cover audio
  - Audio with 1-bit LSB steganography
  - Audio with 4-bit LSB steganography
  - Audio with hash-based (random position) LSB steganography
